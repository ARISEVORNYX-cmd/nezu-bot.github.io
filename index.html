Commands

Common Nezu bot commands.

CommandDescription
/pingCheck the bot's response time.
/kickRemove a member from the server.
/banBan a member from the server.
/muteTemporarily mute a member.
/clearDelete multiple messages at once.
/serverinfoView server details and statistics.
/userinfoView a member's profile information.
