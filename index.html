Good to know

Quick answers.

Honest notes about permissions and setup.

Is Nezuko Helper free?

The provided bot page describes the bot as free to add and use. Hosting and third-party services can have their own limits or costs.

How do I get started?

Invite the bot to a server you manage, review permissions, and explore its enabled features.

Why does the invite request Administrator?

The invite URL supplied by the owner requests Administrator access. That is broad access. Only approve it for a server you own or manage and trust the bot in.

Can I change its permissions later?

Server admins can edit the bot’s role permissions in Server Settings → Roles. Removing permissions may disable some features.

Is the bot always online?

Website availability does not prove the Discord process is online. Check the live bot and hosting console for current status.

How do I report a problem?

Use the bot’s report/support tools if available, or ask in Nezuko HQ. Never share tokens, passwords or OTPs.
